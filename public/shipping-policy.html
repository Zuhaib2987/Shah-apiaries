1. Where we deliver

We deliver to most PIN codes in India. We do not ship outside India yet. If a courier cannot service your PIN code, we will contact you to find another option or cancel with a full refund.

2. Dispatch and delivery times

StepUsual time
Packing and dispatch1 to 2 working days after the order is confirmed (after payment or the COD advance)
Jammu & Kashmir2 to 4 days after dispatch
Rest of India4 to 8 days after dispatch
North-East, Ladakh, islands and remote areas6 to 10 days after dispatch

These are estimates, not guarantees. Weather, snowfall, road closures, holidays, curfews, internet shutdowns and courier delays can add time, especially in winter. Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

3. Shipping charges

  • Free shipping on orders of ₹999 and above, after discounts.
  • Below that, a flat ₹80 per order.
  • Cash on delivery adds a COD fee of ₹40 and needs an online advance of 50% (20% in Jammu & Kashmir). See our terms.
  • All charges are shown at checkout before you pay. There are no hidden fees.

4. Tracking

Once your parcel ships, we send the courier name and tracking number on WhatsApp. You can also see it under My orders on our website using your order number and mobile number.

5. Address and delivery

  • Please give a full address with landmark, PIN code and a reachable mobile number. We are not responsible for delays or loss caused by an incomplete or wrong address.
  • To change the address, message us before dispatch.
  • Check the parcel at delivery. If the box is visibly damaged or wet, note it with the courier if you can.
  • Always record an unboxing video when you open the parcel. It is required for any damage, leakage, wrong or missing item claim, which must reach us within 24 hours of delivery.

6. Delays, lost and damaged parcels

  • If tracking has not moved for 5 working days, tell us and we will chase the courier.
  • If a parcel is confirmed lost in transit, we will resend it or refund you in full.
  • Broken or leaking jars are replaced or refunded under our Refunds & cancellations policy.
