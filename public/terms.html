4. Health and safety

  • Do not give honey to infants under 12 months, because of the risk of infant botulism.
  • Honey contains natural pollen and bee products. If you are allergic to pollen, bee stings or bee products, consult a doctor before eating it.
  • Honey is a sugar. People with diabetes or on medical diets should follow their doctor's advice.
  • Anything on this site about the uses or traditions of honey is general information, not medical advice, and our products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.
  • Store jars closed, at room temperature, away from sunlight and moisture, and use a dry spoon. Use before the best before date on the label.
