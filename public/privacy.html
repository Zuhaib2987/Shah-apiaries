This policy explains what personal data Shah Apiaries™ ("we", "us") collects when you visit shahapiaries.in or buy from us, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

In short:

  • We collect only what we need to deliver your honey and support your order.
  • We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details. Razorpay handles payments.
  • We do not sell your data, rent it, or use it for advertising profiles.
  • We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs to do its job, and never sell your data.

  • Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. processes online and advance payments under its own privacy policy and RBI rules.
  • Courier and postal partners (for example India Post, Delhivery, DTDC or others we use) receive your name, phone and address to deliver your parcel.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the website and stores order records in its database service. Its servers may be located outside India.
  • Google serves the website's fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Meta (WhatsApp and Instagram) carries any messages you exchange with us on those apps.
  • Government and legal authorities when the law requires it, for example tax authorities, courts or police with a valid order.
  • A buyer or successor if the business is ever sold or transferred, under the same protections described here.

These partners must keep your data secure and use it only for the services they provide to us.

5. Transfers outside India

Some of our service providers, such as Cloudflare and Google, may process data on servers outside India. We use them only in ways permitted under the DPDP Act, 2023 and will follow any restrictions the Government of India notifies on transfers to particular countries.

7. How we protect it

  • The whole site runs over encrypted HTTPS.
  • Payments are handled entirely on Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant checkout.
  • Only the owner and one family member can open the order dashboard, with hashed passwords, secure session cookies and automatic blocking after repeated wrong logins.
  • Order tracking needs both the order number and the mobile number used for the order.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens, we will inform affected customers and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 you can:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we shared it with.
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data, such as a changed address.
  • Erase: ask us to delete your data, unless we must keep it by law, for example invoices.
  • Withdraw consent at any time. This will not affect what was done before, and we may not be able to complete an order that is still in progress.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to do so.
  • Grievance redressal: complain to our Grievance Officer, and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

Email us from the address or WhatsApp number linked to your order so we can confirm it is you. We reply within 7 days and act on requests within 30 days.

9. Cookies and local storage

  • The shop sets no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • Your browser's local storage keeps your cart, wishlist, saved address and recent orders, as described in section 2.
  • Our order dashboard uses one essential security cookie for staff logins only.
  • Razorpay may set its own cookies inside the payment window to process and secure your payment.

10. Children

Our shop is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please order with the involvement of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children without verifiable parental consent, track children, or target them with advertising. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Messages from us

12. Other websites

13. Changes to this policy
