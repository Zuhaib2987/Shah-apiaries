Our story

A family apiary, not a bottling plant

Shah Apiaries™ is our family's beekeeping, run from Babapora, Zoonimar in Srinagar. In spring our colonies work the valley's acacia; in summer they move up to the wildflower meadows of Gurez. We jar only what our own bees make.

  1. Hives on the moveColonies follow the bloom from valley orchards to high meadows.
  2. Harvest capped frames onlyWe take honey only once bees have sealed it, so moisture stays low.
  3. Settle and strainCold strained through cloth. No heating, no blending, no added sugar.
  4. Test each batchA sample from every batch goes to an FSSAI-recognised lab.
  5. Jar and codePacked by hand in Srinagar with a batch code you can look up above.
