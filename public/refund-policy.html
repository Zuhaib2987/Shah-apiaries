If something goes wrong

Refunds & cancellations

Last updated 20 October 2026

Honey is a food product, so for safety we cannot take back opened or used jars. But if anything is wrong with your order, we will make it right.

1. Cancelling an order

  • Before dispatch: cancel any time by WhatsApp or email with your order number. You get a full refund of everything paid, including any COD advance.
  • After dispatch: the order can no longer be cancelled. You may refuse delivery, in which case the refund rules for refused orders below apply.
  • Custom, bulk and gift orders with personalised tags or notes can be cancelled for a full refund only before we start preparing them. After that, the advance covers materials already used.
  • We may cancel an order ourselves, as described in our terms. If we do, you get a full refund.

2. Damaged, leaking or wrong items

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo or, ideally, an unboxing video showing the problem and the shipping label.
  • If a jar arrived broken or leaking, or you received the wrong honey or size, we will send a free replacement or give a full refund for that item, whichever you prefer.
  • If something is missing from your order, we will send it or refund it.
  • You do not need to return a broken jar. For a wrong item, we may arrange a free pickup or ask you to keep it.

3. Quality concerns

If you believe your honey is not as described, tell us within 7 days of delivery with your batch code and jar number. We will check it against that batch's lab report and, if there is a genuine problem, replace it or refund it.

The following are natural features of raw honey and are not defects: crystallisation or setting, separation into layers, foam on top, tiny specks of pollen or wax, and differences in colour or taste from the photos or from a previous batch.

4. What we cannot refund

  • Opened or partly used jars, unless the problem existed when it was delivered.
  • Change of mind after dispatch.
  • Problems reported after 48 hours (damage) or 7 days (quality) without a good reason.
  • Damage caused after delivery, for example from wrong storage.

5. Refused or undelivered orders

If a parcel comes back to us because delivery was refused, the address was wrong or incomplete, or you could not be reached after the courier's attempts:

  • Prepaid orders: we refund the amount paid minus the actual shipping charges both ways, or reship it if you pay the shipping again.
  • COD orders: we refund the advance minus the actual shipping charges both ways and the COD fee of ₹40.

6. How and when refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are started within 2 working days.
  • Online and advance payments are refunded to the original payment method through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show it.
  • Cash paid on delivery is refunded by UPI or bank transfer to an account you give us.
  • Any discount or offer on the order is adjusted in proportion to the refunded items.
  • We will share the refund reference number so you can follow it up with your bank.
